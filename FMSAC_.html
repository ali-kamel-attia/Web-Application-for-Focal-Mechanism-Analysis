Frohlich triangle: the three corners are pure normal (P axis vertical), thrust (T vertical) and strike-slip (B vertical) faulting. A point is placed by sin² of the plunge of each axis (the three values sum to 1). A mechanism is Normal / Thrust / Strike-slip when that axis plunges at least the threshold; otherwise it is oblique, named after its steepest axis (P: normal-oblique, T: thrust-oblique, B: odd).
